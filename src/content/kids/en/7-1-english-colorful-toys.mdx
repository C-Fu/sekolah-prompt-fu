## Challenge 4: Prompt Doctor — Fix the Toy Request 🩺

Peromfu heard an impolite, confusing command: *"Give toys"*. Let's heal it so the AI shopkeeper can understand and respond politely!

<PromptDoctor
  title="Heal the Toy Prompt"
