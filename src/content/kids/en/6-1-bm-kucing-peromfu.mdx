## Challenge 4: Prompt Doctor — Fix the Kitten Prompt 🩺

Peromfu saw a friend write a very weak prompt: *"Draw kitten"*. Let's heal it so the AI knows what to illustrate!

  title="Heal the Kitten Prompt"
  brokenPrompt="Draw kitten"
