# Discussion Log: Phase 24 - Outer Planets Subject Architecture & Telemetry

## Date: 2026-11-19
## Attendees: User & Orchestrator Agent

### Agenda
1. Naming convention for outer planets: whether to rename planet IDs to align with subjects (`<planet>-<subject>`) or keep `-astronomy`.
2. Thematic color palette preservation and assignment for KSSR subjects.
3. Bilingual title and tagline style for Planetary HUD and learning badges.

### Outcomes
- **Decision 1 (Planet IDs):** Approved updating planet IDs to `jupiter-sains`, `saturn-sejarah`, `uranus-rbt`, and `neptune-psv`, keeping legacy compatibility where necessary.
- **Decision 2 (Palette):** Approved retaining and locking the shader palettes:
  - Jupiter: Amber / Storm Bronze (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)
  - Saturn: Golden Ochre (`#EAB308` / `#FDE047`)
  - Uranus: Cyan / Blueprint Teal (`#06B6D4` / `#22D3EE`)
  - Neptune: Royal Azure (`#2563EB` / `#3B82F6`)
- **Decision 3 (Titles & Taglines):** Approved full bilingual subject titles (`Sains & Penerokaan Saintifik`, `Sejarah & Garis Masa`, `Reka Bentuk & Teknologi`, `Pendidikan Seni Visual`) with rich pedagogy-focused taglines in both BM and EN.
