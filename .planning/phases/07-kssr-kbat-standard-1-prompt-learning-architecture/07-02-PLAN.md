# Plan 07-02: Define Planetary World Spec and Level Schema for KSSR Subjects

## Objective
Define the data schema, route architecture, and cartographic layout for the planetary subject worlds (Moon, Earth, Mars, Venus, Mercury) and their Standard 1 regions based on the mockups in `planets_mockup/`.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Planetary Domain & Cartography Schema
- Model planets in TypeScript configuration (`src/lib/kids/planets.ts` or architectural spec):
  - Planet metadata: `id`, `name`, `nameMs`, `celestialBody`, `subject`, `subjectMs`, `colorTheme`, `texture/svgKey`.
  - Feature/Continent mapping: Year 1 entry regions (e.g. `Oceanus Procellarum`, `Elysium Planitia`, `Ishtar Terra`, `Borealis Planitia`).
  - Final Boss landmarks (Olympus Mons, Caloris Basin, Maxwell Montes, South Pole-Aitken) flagged as locked until higher milestones.

### 2. KSSR Level Metadata Schema Extension
- Extend or align existing level types (`src/lib/kids/levels.ts`) to support planetary curriculum levels:
  - Subject tagging (`subject: "bm" | "sains" | "matematik" | "english" | "moral"`).
  - Standard/Year tagging (`standard: 1`).
  - DSKP code & KBAT skill references (`kbatSkill: "menganalisis" | "menilai" | "mencipta"`).
  - Dual-locale slug mapping and loader contracts.

### 3. Pilot Level Specification
- Specify the 5 pilot levels (1 per subject) to be authored and tested in Phase 08:
  - BM-1: *Peromfu & Kucing Comel* (Bina ayat dengan kata nama & kata kerja).
  - Sains-1: *Robot Jumpa Benda Hidup* (Klasifikasi benda hidup vs bukan hidup).
  - Matematik-1: *Kira Bintang di Marikh* (Operasi tambah asas dengan kekangan format).
  - English-1: *Peromfu's Colorful Toys* (Describing classroom objects with colors).
  - Moral-1: *Sahabat Sejati Peromfu* (Prompting AI to act kindly and help a classmate).

## Deliverables
- `.planning/phases/07-kssr-kbat-standard-1-prompt-learning-architecture/PLANETARY_ARCHITECTURE.md` with complete TypeScript interfaces, route diagrams, and pilot level design blueprints.
