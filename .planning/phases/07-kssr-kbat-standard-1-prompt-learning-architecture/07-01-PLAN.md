# Plan 07-01: Research and Formalize KSSR Semakan Year 1 DSKP Mapping & Pedagogy

## Objective
Research the Malaysian Ministry of Education (MOE) KSSR Semakan Standard 1 / Tahun 1 DSKP (Dokumen Standard Kurikulum dan Pentaksiran) across the 5 target subjects (Bahasa Melayu, Sains, Matematik, English, Pendidikan Moral) and formalize the prompt-engineering learning matrix, mapping each subject unit and KBAT cognitive domain to prompt-based game mechanics.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Research & Document DSKP Learning Standards
- Extract authentic Standard 1 learning standards:
  - **BM (Bulan / Moon):** DSKP 2.1 (Membaca perkataan, frasa, ayat), DSKP 3.2 (Menulis perkataan/frasa), DSKP 5.1 (Golongan kata: kata nama am/khas, kata kerja).
  - **Sains (Bumi / Earth):** DSKP 3.1 (Benda Hidup & Bukan Hidup), DSKP 4.1 (Deria Manusia), DSKP 5.1 (Bahagian tubuh haiwan).
  - **Matematik (Marikh / Mars):** DSKP 1.1 (Kuantiti secara intuitif & membilang), DSKP 1.2 (Nilai nombor hingga 100), DSKP 2.1 (Operasi asas tambah/tolak), DSKP 7.1 (Bentuk dua dimensi).
  - **English (Zuhrah / Venus):** DSKP 1.1 (Listen to and recognize sounds), DSKP 2.1 (Communicate simple information - colors, classroom, greetings).
  - **Pendidikan Moral (Utarid / Mercury):** DSKP 1.1 (Nilai kepercayaan kepada Tuhan), DSKP 2.1 (Kebaikan & tolong-menolong), DSKP 5.1 (Kesopanan dan budi pekerti).

### 2. Map KBAT Cognition & Prompt Engineering Competencies
- Create pedagogical specification:
  - **Prompt Fundamentals:** Clarity (Kejelasan), Specificity (Ketepatan/Perincian), Constraints (Kekangan/Had perkataan atau format), Role-playing (Peranan/Persona), Verification (Menyemak jawapan AI).
  - **KBAT Bloom's Taxonomy for Year 1:**
    - *Mengingat & Memahami:* Recognizing prompt patterns with `WordPredictor`.
    - *Mengaplikasi & Menganalisis:* Diagnosing incorrect or vague AI outputs with `PromptDoctor` and `PromptVsMistake`.
    - *Menilai & Mencipta:* Assembling complete, constraint-abiding prompts with `DragDropPrompt`.
