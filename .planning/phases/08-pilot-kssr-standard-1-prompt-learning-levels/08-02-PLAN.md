# Plan 08-02: Wire KSSR Pilot Levels into Registry, Loaders, i18n & Tests

## Objective
Wire the 5 new KSSR Standard 1 pilot levels (Worlds 6 through 10) into `src/lib/kids/levels.ts`, `src/lib/kids/level-content.ts`, and i18n message catalogs (`messages/ms.json` & `messages/en.json`). Update tests to reflect the 24 registered level routes and certify all quality gates.
