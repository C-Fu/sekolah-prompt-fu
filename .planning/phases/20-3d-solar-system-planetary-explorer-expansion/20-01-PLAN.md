# Plan 20-01: Expand 3D Solar System and Cartography scenes with all 9 bodies and Saturn rings

## Context
Expand the 3D Three.js Solar System scene and individual planet cartography scene to support all 9 bodies in `PLANET_WORLDS`: Mercury, Venus, Earth with orbiting Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn with lightweight ring geometry, Uranus with procedural atmosphere, and Neptune.

## Tasks
1. **Expand `solar-system-scene.ts`:**
   - Update `primaryConfigs` to include all outer planets: Jupiter (dist 26.0, r 1.4, `jupiter.jpg`), Saturn (dist 32.0, r 1.2, `saturn.jpg`), Uranus (dist 39.0, r 0.95, procedural), Neptune (dist 45.0, r 0.9, `neptune.jpg`).
   - Add Saturn ring geometry (`THREE.RingGeometry(1.6, 2.6, 48)`) attached to Saturn mesh with 26.7° tilt and minimal CPU double-sided material.
   - Ensure proper cleanup and texture disposal in `dispose()`.
2. **Update `planet-cartography-scene.ts`:**
   - Support texture loading for all 9 planets in `getPlanetTexturePath`.
   - Add Saturn rings in the close-up cartography scene if inspecting Saturn.
3. **Verify Explorer Transitions:**
   - Ensure clicking any planet in `solar-system-scene` zooms to that planet and mounts the cartography view smoothly.
4. **Update Tests:**
   - Update `solar-system-scene.test.ts` to verify 9 planet meshes, Saturn ring mesh, orbit tracks, and cleanup.

## Verification
- Run `vitest run src/__tests__/kids/solar-system-scene.test.ts src/__tests__/kids/planetary-explorer.test.tsx`.
