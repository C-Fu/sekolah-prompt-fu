Here is the full set compiled with strict, sequential numbering from 1 through 10, without resetting:

1. The High-Density Proxmox / Enterprise Hypervisor Aesthetic
Best fit for an actual self-hosted hardware simulator. It communicates serious, no-nonsense enterprise engineering rather than a flashy consumer landing page.

* Vibe: VMware ESXi, Grafana, Proxmox VE 8, Datadog dense dashboards.
* Palette:
* Canvas: Neutral pitch-black or charcoal (bg-neutral-950 / #0a0a0a), NOT tinted slate blue.
* Panels: Pure dark grey (bg-neutral-900 / #171717) separated by crisp, subtle borders (border-neutral-800).
* Accents: Industrial orange/amber (text-amber-500, bg-amber-500/10) or phosphor green (#22c55e) for status dots.


* Typography & Geometry:
* Strict tabular typography: Monospace numerals and labels (font-mono tracking-tight text-xs uppercase).
* Micro-radii: Flatten the overly round corners to sharp 2px or 4px (rounded-sm).
* Remove pill badges; use plain uppercase text chips with a simple pipe or square status beacon: [■ COLLABORATION] instead of rounded colored badges.



---

2. Monochromatic Swiss / Neo-Brutalist Minimal
Gives the platform an intentional, bespoke developer feel like Vercel, Linear, or Raycast, eliminating the "AI bootstrap theme" vibe entirely.

* Vibe: High-contrast, sharp gridlines, typographic hierarchy doing all the visual lifting.
* Palette:
* Canvas: Stark black (#000000) or off-black (#0d0d0d).
* Borders: Visible hairline grid dividers (border border-white/10).
* Text: Bright white (text-white) for headings, high-contrast muted grey (text-neutral-400) for body. Zero gradient fills on cards.
* Accent: Single-color functional highlight—pure white buttons with black text (bg-white text-black hover:bg-neutral-200) or international Klein blue (#2563eb).


* Typography & Geometry:
* Clean geometric grotesk font (Geist, Inter Tight, or Cabinet Grotesk).
* Ditch card shadows and backdrop-blur glows completely; razor-sharp structural hairline boxes.

* Palette:
* Canvas: Cold carbon black (#050806 / #090d0a) with faint cathode scanlines.
* Surfaces: Inset CRT terminal bezels and dark olive panels (#0d140f) framed by deep phosphor borders (#162a1d).
* Accents: Phosphor terminal green (#00ff66) with soft raster glow, paired with safety telemetry amber (#ffb000) for status alerts.


* Typography & Geometry:
* Typography: Monospace terminal stacks (Share Tech Mono, VT323) alongside high-contrast tech sans (Chakra Petch) for subsystem labels.
* Geometry: Inset beveled screens (shadow-inner), segmented digital LED bar meters, and zero rounded pill badges in favor of raw text chips like [CH-01: ACTIVE].
