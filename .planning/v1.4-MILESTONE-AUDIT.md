## Executive Summary

Milestone **v1.4: Planetary Assets, Data, Solar System Explorer Expansion & Allo Typography** successfully expanded Sekolah Prompt-Fu's 3D planetary explorer (`/planets`) with authentic astronomical textures, dimensions, and observational states adapted from `how-big-is-big`, while preserving all core KSSR MDX lesson structures and 35 geological features across Earth, Moon, Mars, Venus, and Mercury. In addition, Josh Puckett's **Allo font** was self-hosted and implemented universally across the entire application, achieving a cheerful, playful, and cheeky aesthetic with balanced roundness (`ROND: 50`) that delights kids while maintaining clean readability for adult educators.

All 14 scoped requirements have been implemented, tested, and certified with 100% static export (`31/31` pages prerendered).
