## Executive Summary

Milestone **v1.5: Outer Planets Curriculum Expansion (KSSR Sains, Sejarah, RBT, PSV)** successfully expanded Sekolah Prompt-Fu to the outer solar system. It transformed Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune from astronomical observation placeholders into active KSSR learning worlds. 

Four complete Year 1 (Tahun 1) interactive pilot lessons across 7 canonical stages were authored with rich prompt engineering widgets (`WordPredictor`, `PromptVsMistake`, `PromptDoctor`, `DragDropPrompt`, `MagicWords`, `LevelComplete`) and bilingual symmetry (Bahasa Melayu and English). 28 authentic atmospheric and geological features were mapped across the outer planets (total 63 features across the solar system), and total levels in the application were expanded from 23 to 27.

All 11 scoped requirements have been implemented, tested, and certified with 100% static export (`35/35` pages and `27/27` static level routes prerendered).
